ShortsStudio

A private video-publishing tool for our own YouTube channels.

ShortsStudio is a personal application that prepares short educational videos (Islamic texts and duas with translations, history, facts, news summaries, children's songs and more) and uploads them to YouTube channels that we own and operate, using the YouTube Data API. It is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's YouTube account.

What it does with YouTube

  • Uploads finished videos, with their title, description and tags, to the owner's own channels.
  • Sets the video privacy / scheduled publish time and the "made for kids" and altered-content declarations.
  • Reads the owner's channel name and ID to make sure each video goes to the right channel, and reads YouTube's public trending chart to choose topics for original commentary videos.
