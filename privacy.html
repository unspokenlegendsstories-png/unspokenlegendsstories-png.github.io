Privacy Policy

ShortsStudio · Last updated: 21 October 2026

Who we are

ShortsStudio is a private application run by the owner of the YouTube channels it publishes to. Contact: unspokenlegends.stories@gmail.com.

YouTube API Services

ShortsStudio uses YouTube API Services. By using ShortsStudio you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What data we access

  • Google account authorization (OAuth tokens) for the channel owner's own YouTube channels, with the scopes "upload videos" and "view your YouTube account".
  • Channel name and channel ID of those channels, to send each video to the correct channel.
  • Public YouTube data such as the public trending videos chart (titles, channel names, view counts), used only to choose topics for our own original videos.

We do not access, collect or store data about any other YouTube users or viewers.

How we use and store it

  • Authorization tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer and are used only to upload and manage the owner's own videos.
  • We do not sell, share, rent or transfer any data to third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.
  • Data is kept only as long as the channel is connected. Removing the connection deletes the stored tokens.

How to revoke access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. Revoking access stops ShortsStudio from uploading.

Cookies

This website does not use cookies or tracking.

Changes

We will update this page if anything changes, and show the new date at the top.
